### Alpha Calibration Lookup Table (Double the Workforce)
| If you double your workforce (2x workers), and the task finishes in... | Then your Alpha (α) is approximately... | Example Trade |
|---|---|---|
| 50% of original time (Perfect efficiency) | 1.0 | Trench digging, basic labor |
| 60% of original time | 0.7 - 0.8 | Bricklaying, framing |
| 70% of original time | 0.5 | Electrical wiring, plumbing |
| 85% of original time (Terrible efficiency) | 0.2 - 0.3 | Highly confined spaces, elevator shaft install |
